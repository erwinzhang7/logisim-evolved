## Upstream artwork reused in this port

`swift/Sources/LogisimUI/Icons/ToolIcons.swift` is a transcription of upstream *artwork*, not a
translation of upstream logic. The logic-gate, Select, wiring and Pin glyphs shown in the
toolbar and component explorer are logisim-evolution's own icon drawings, read out of the
shipped 4.1.0 jar with `javap -c -p` and rewritten as Swift vector geometry,
`com.cburch.logisim.std.gates.AbstractGate.paintIconANSI` and `paintIconBufferAnsi`, plus the
matching painters in `com.cburch.logisim.gui.icons`, `com.cburch.logisim.tools` and
`com.cburch.logisim.std.wiring`. Each glyph names its source method in its own doc comment.

Both works are GPL-3.0-only, so the reuse needs no separate grant, but it is upstream's design
work and is credited as such, in this file, in the bundled `NOTICE.md` and in **About ▸
Credits ▸ Artwork**.

The application's own mark (`AboutWindow.AboutMark`) and app icon are **not** upstream's logo,
and upstream's logo is not redistributed here. A project's name and logo can carry trade-mark
rights that a copyright licence does not convey, and GPLv3 section 7(e) expressly contemplates a
term about them; rather than rely on a reading of anyone else's rights, this port simply uses its
own mark, its own name, its own bundle identifier and its own document UTI.

## Known divergences from upstream 4.1.0

Java component libraries (`.jar`), the vendor FPGA toolchains (Xilinx ISE and Vivado, Intel
Quartus) and the TCL console are not available in this build. Circuits that use them still
open, and their components are preserved exactly on save rather than being silently discarded.
